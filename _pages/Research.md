# Research

My academic interests centre on actuarial science, pension risk, quantitative finance, and statistical learning, with a focus on methods that can support long-term financial decisions under uncertainty.

## Deep Q-Learning for Defined Contribution Pension Investment

**Bachelor's thesis · University of Amsterdam · 2026**

This project studies dynamic portfolio allocation during the accumulation phase of a defined contribution pension arrangement with an inflation-indexed return guarantee. It applies **Deep Q-Networks (DQN)** to compare investment strategies while accounting for the risk of falling below a guaranteed pension value.

The numerical analysis uses monthly stock, bond, and short-term interest-rate proxies. Learning-based strategies are compared with an all-equity allocation, a 60/40 portfolio, and a lifecycle strategy. Evaluation includes out-of-sample testing and stressed market scenarios.

The thesis was awarded a **9/10**.

**Research code:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/hyzhao0916/DC-pension-DQN-python-code)

### Research directions

I am interested in further developing this work through richer allocation spaces, additional asset classes, alternative risk measures, and more robust reinforcement-learning methods.
