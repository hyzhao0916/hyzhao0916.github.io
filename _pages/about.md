# About me

I am a master's student in **Actuarial Science and Mathematical Finance** at the **University of Amsterdam (UvA)** and a **Working Student at Milliman Benelux**, focusing on pensions.

My interests lie at the intersection of actuarial science, quantitative finance, risk management, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in how statistical and computational methods can improve long-term financial decision-making and pension design.

I completed my BSc in Actuarial Science at UvA in 2026. My bachelor's thesis investigated **deep reinforcement learning for investment strategies in defined contribution pension schemes with return guarantees**, earning a grade of **9/10**.

## Research interests

- Pension economics and defined contribution retirement systems
- Actuarial risk modelling and risk management
- Reinforcement learning and quantitative investment strategies
- Statistical and computational methods in finance

## Selected research

**Deep Q-Learning for Defined Contribution Pension Investment** (BSc thesis, 2026)

I developed and evaluated Deep Q-Network (DQN) strategies for portfolio allocation in defined contribution pension accumulation, including an inflation-indexed contribution guarantee. I compared learning-based policies with static and lifecycle investment benchmarks and evaluated shortfall risk under different market scenarios.

[Read more about the research](/research/) · [View Python notebooks on GitHub](https://github.com/hyzhao0916/DC-pension-DQN-python-code)
